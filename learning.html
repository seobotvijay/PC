Frequently asked questions

What does the Learning section cover?

Learning covers academics and exam preparation, building real-world skills, special education needs like dyslexia, and how to handle technology and screens for schoolwork.

Does ParentCircle help with exam stress?

Yes — see Helping Your Child Through Exam Season, Without the Panic below for practical, non-panicked ways to support your child through board exams.

What if my child has a learning difference?

The Special Education topic covers conditions like dyslexia and dyscalculia; for a specific concern, it’s worth also talking to your child’s school and a specialist.
