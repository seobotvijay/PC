    What happens when my child moves to the next stage?

    Change your stage any time from your profile. Nothing is locked, and your saved articles and community history stay with you.

    How does the free trial work?

    Seven days of Pro. Cancel before day seven and you are not charged. We send a reminder on day five.

    No. It answers everyday parenting questions using ParentCircle’s own expert-reviewed content, and it will tell you when something needs a paediatrician or a counsellor.
