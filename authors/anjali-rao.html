Anjali Rao

Certified Positive Parenting Coach

Child Behaviour Positive Parenting Discipline Tantrums

Anjali coaches parents through the everyday behaviour challenges of raising toddlers and school-age children — tantrums, defiance, sibling fights, and the exhausting business of holding a limit at 6pm. A mother of two herself, she draws on positive-parenting frameworks and her own coaching practice.

Her writing for ParentCircle focuses on practical, judgment-free guidance: what is actually happening behind a behaviour, and what to try instead of what you were taught growing up.
