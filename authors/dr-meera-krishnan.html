Dr. Meera Krishnan

Child Psychologist, M.Phil Clinical Psychology

Confidence Anxiety Emotional Regulation Parent-Child Relationship

Meera works with children and families on confidence, anxiety and emotional regulation. She has run parent workshops across schools for over eight years, alongside a private practice supporting children through school transitions, friendship difficulties and big feelings.

She writes for ParentCircle on raising emotionally secure children — how confidence is actually built day to day, and the small, repeatable habits that help more than a single big conversation ever could.
