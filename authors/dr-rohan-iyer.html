Dr. Rohan Iyer

Paediatrician, MBBS, DCH

Baby & Child Health Sleep Screen Time Vaccinations

Rohan is a paediatrician who has spent over a decade helping families navigate everyday child health questions — feeding, sleep, fevers, and increasingly, screen habits. He runs a general paediatric practice and sees the same questions come up again and again in clinic, which is what he writes about for ParentCircle.

His approach leans practical over prescriptive: fewer hard rules, more starting points parents can adapt to their own family. He is a strong believer that most parenting problems are better solved with one consistent small habit than with a big one-time intervention.
