Questions

Frequently asked questions

What does the Wellness section cover?

Wellness covers physical health and fitness, common illnesses, developmental milestones, and children’s mental health — stress, anxiety and emotional wellbeing.

Where can I find mental health resources for my child?

See the Mental Health topic below, and for a clinical concern, India’s NIMHANS (National Institute of Mental Health and Neurosciences) is a trusted starting point.

Does ParentCircle wellness content replace medical advice?

No. It is general guidance to help you understand a situation — for anything specific to your child, speak to your paediatrician.
