What is ParentCircle

A parenting companion, not another feed to scroll.

ParentCircle is a parenting platform built for parents in India, raising children from birth through the teenage years. Instead of generic advice, everything here is organized by your child’s exact age and the specific thing you’re dealing with today — picky eating, a screen-time fight, exam pressure, or just wanting to understand your own parenting style better.

We combine expert-reviewed articles, quick interactive tools and a community of parents who are living the same daily moments as you — so the advice feels less like a lecture and more like a friend who’s been there.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about how ParentCircle works.

What is ParentCircle?

ParentCircle is a parenting platform for Indian families that organizes expert-reviewed articles, interactive tools and community discussions by your child’s exact age, from infancy through the teenage years.

Is ParentCircle free to use?

Yes. ParentCircle’s articles, the Poll of the Day, the parenting-style quiz and the parenting-tips tool are all free to use, with no sign-up required.

How does the Know Your Parenting Style quiz work?

You answer 12 short, everyday situations written for your child’s age band. In about 90 seconds you get a reflection on your parenting style, what you’re doing well, and one or two things to try this week. It is a self-reflection tool, not a clinical assessment.

Who writes ParentCircle’s parenting tips and articles?

Content is contributed by paediatricians, child psychologists, educators and parenting coaches, alongside real stories and questions from the ParentCircle parent community.

What ages does ParentCircle cover?

ParentCircle covers every stage from infants (0–1 year) through toddlers, pre-schoolers, primary-school children, pre-teens and teenagers up to 18, plus general guidance for parents of adult children.

Do I need to create an account to use the tools?

No. The Poll of the Day, the parenting-style quiz and the parenting-tips tool all work without an account. Your poll vote and quiz result are stored only in your own browser, not on a server.
