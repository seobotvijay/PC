What is ParentCircle

A parenting companion, not another feed to scroll.

ParentCircle is a parenting platform built for parents in India, raising children from birth through the teenage years. Instead of generic advice, everything here is organized by your child’s exact age and the specific thing you’re dealing with today — picky eating, a screen-time fight, exam pressure, or just wanting to understand your own parenting style better.

We combine expert-reviewed articles, quick interactive tools and a community of parents who are living the same daily moments as you — so the advice feels less like a lecture and more like a friend who’s been there.
