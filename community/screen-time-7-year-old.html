Screen Time

How many hours of screen time is actually okay for a 7-year-old on a school day?

P Priya · Mother of a 7-year-old

My 7-year-old wants his tablet the second he’s home from school and it turns into a whole negotiation every single day. Some days I let him have an hour, other days it stretches to two because I’m too tired to fight about it. Am I the only one who feels like this is a losing battle? What actually works for you?

3 replies from the community

K Kavita · Mother of two

We do a hard 45 minutes right after homework, non-negotiable, using a visible kitchen timer. The timer takes the blame off me — it’s not “mom said stop”, it’s the timer.

A Arjun · Father of a 9-year-old

Honestly we stopped counting hours and started looking at what it’s replacing. If he’s still playing outside and sleeping fine, I don’t stress the exact number as much.

M Meenal · Mother of an 8-year-old

Following this thread! Same fight here. Curious what other parents do specifically on weekends, since our rules kind of fall apart on Saturdays.
