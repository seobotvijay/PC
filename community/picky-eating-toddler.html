Picky Eating

Any real tricks for a toddler who refuses anything green?

S Sneha · Mother of a 2-year-old

My 2-year-old used to eat everything and now suddenly anything green gets pushed off the plate immediately, no tasting, nothing. Dinner has become a battle and I’m running out of ideas.

3 replies from the community

F Farah · Mother of a toddler

We stopped commenting on what she ate at all, and oddly she started trying more on her own within a couple of weeks.

R Ritu · Mother of two

Blending spinach into parathas worked for us, but honestly it took about fifteen tries before he’d even touch a new vegetable plain.

V Vishal · Father of a 3-year-old

This is such a normal toddler phase. It will probably pass, and come back a few more times before age 5!
