Sibling Rivalry

New baby at home and my 5-year-old has become impossible — is this normal?

A Ananya · Mother of a 5-year-old and a newborn

Since the baby arrived three weeks ago, my older one has started talking back, refusing to listen, and having meltdowns over tiny things. She was never like this before. Is this just an adjustment phase?

3 replies from the community

K Karthik · Father of two

Completely normal — we went through the exact same thing. Giving our older one 15 minutes of one-on-one time daily, just the two of us, made a huge difference within a couple of weeks.

L Lakshmi · Mother of two

Involving her in baby care — fetching diapers, “helping” feed — made her feel important instead of replaced.

N Nisha · Mother of three

It gets easier! Ours settled down by about the two-month mark.
