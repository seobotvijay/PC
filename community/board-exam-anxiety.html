Board Exams

My Class 10 child is anxious about the boards — how do I help without adding to the pressure?

R Rekha · Mother of a Class 10 student

My son barely eats before his practice tests and says his stomach hurts most mornings now. I don’t want to add pressure but I also don’t know how much of this is normal nerves versus something I should be more worried about.

3 replies from the community

S Suresh · Father of a Class 10 student

We started a 20-minute walk together every evening, no talk about studies allowed during it. Made a bigger difference than I expected.

D Divya · Mother of a Class 12 student

My daughter went through this too. What helped was NOT asking “how did the test go” the second she walked in the door.

A Anand · Father of two

If the stomach aches are daily and not just before big tests, it might be worth a quick chat with your paediatrician, just to rule things out and get some reassurance.
